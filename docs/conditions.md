# Physiotherapist Management of COPD, Asthma, CABG, and Heart Failure

Consolidated from GOLD 2025, GINA 2025, ATS 2023 PR Clinical Practice Guideline, AACVPR Guidelines for CR/PR Programs, ACC/AHA 2022 HF Guideline, ESC 2021 + 2023 HF Focused Update, ATS 2013 EIB CPG, ATS 2002 / ERS-ATS 2014 6MWT standards, AACVPR/ACSM exercise testing termination criteria, and peer-reviewed PR/CR literature.

---

## Condition: COPD

### 1. Pathophysiology relevant to physio
- Expiratory airflow limitation from small airway disease + emphysema. **Dynamic hyperinflation** during exercise is the dominant mechanical reason patients stop. Operating lung volumes shift toward TLC → increased work of breathing and "air hunger."
- **V/Q mismatch** drives exertional desaturation; **peripheral muscle dysfunction** (quadriceps atrophy, fiber-type IIx shift, reduced oxidative capacity) is partly independent of airflow limitation and is a key training target.
- Comorbidities (GOLD 2025 emphasis): cardiovascular disease, pulmonary HTN, osteoporosis, sarcopenia, anxiety/depression, OSA, metabolic syndrome.

### 2. Assessment battery
- **Subjective:** mMRC (≥2 = "more symptomatic"), CAT (≥10 = more symptomatic), Borg CR-10, sputum, smoking, exacerbation history (≥2 moderate or ≥1 hospitalization = "E" group).
- **Objective:** HR, BP, SpO2, RR, accessory muscle use, auscultation, chest expansion (≥5 cm normal), posture, peripheral edema, finger clubbing, cyanosis.
- **Functional:** 6MWT (use best of two, ATS/ERS 2014), ISWT, 1-min sit-to-stand, 4-m gait speed (<0.8 m/s = frailty), SPPB.
- **Strength:** handgrip, quadriceps 1-RM, MIP/MEP if IMT considered.
- **Same-day red flags:** SpO2 <88% on prescribed O2, HR >120, SBP >180 / <90, DBP >100, fever, acute change in sputum, unstable angina, new arrhythmia.

### 3. Goals
- **Short-term (4 wk):** reduce dyspnea on ADLs, teach PLB and pacing, raise SpO2 stability, ↑6MWD by ≥30 m (MCID 25–35 m).
- **Long-term (8–12 wk+):** ↑6MWD to ≥70% predicted, reduce exacerbations/hospitalizations, ↑CAT by ≥2 (MCID), maintain ≥30 min/day activity, transition to community/home maintenance.

### 4. FITT prescription

**Endurance (cornerstone):** F 3–5 d/wk (≥2 supervised) · I 60–80% peak work rate from CPET, or 70–85% peak speed from 6MWT, or Borg dyspnea/RPE 4–6 · T 20–60 min (start 10–15 with intervals; aim 30 continuous) · Type: treadmill, cycle, Nordic walking, ground walking.

**Resistance:** F 2–3 d/wk · I 50–85% 1-RM (start 50–60%) · 2–4 sets × 6–12 · leg press, knee extension, calf raise, seated row, biceps/triceps, lat pulldown.

**Interval (severely dyspneic):** 30 s @ 80–100% peak : 30 s rest, or 1:1 at 100/40% peak for 20–30 min total.

**Flexibility/balance:** daily stretching of pectorals, hip flexors, hamstrings; balance for falls.

**IMT:** if MIP <60 cmH₂O — 30% MIP × 30 breaths twice daily.

### 5. Session structure
- **Warm-up (5–10):** seated marching, shoulder rolls, low-resistance cycling, PLB drill, posture cueing.
- **Conditioning (30–40):** aerobic + resistance circuit; PLB on exhalation; tripod recovery.
- **Cool-down (5–10):** walking taper, stretches, diaphragmatic breathing.
- **Airway clearance (when sputum):** ACBT cycle (breathing control → 3–4 thoracic expansions → breathing control → 1–2 huffs) ×3–4; autogenic drainage; oscillating PEP (Acapella/Aerobika).
- **Energy conservation:** 4 P's (Plan, Pace, Prioritize, Position); inhaler check; exhale on the effort phase.

### 6. Session-by-session program (8-week / 16 sessions, 2×/wk)

Each session: pre-vitals (HR, BP, SpO2, Borg dyspnea), pre-bronchodilator if prescribed, post-vitals each component, SpO2 nadir logged, O2 titrated to maintain SpO2 ≥88–90%.

- **S1 (Assessment / Education):** 6MWT #1, sit-to-stand baseline, handgrip, CAT, mMRC. PLB (6 reps × 4 sets), diaphragmatic breathing supine 5 min. Inhaler technique, action plan. No conditioning. Borg ≤3.
- **S2 (Baseline):** 6MWT #2 (best of two used). Treadmill 10 min @ 70% 6MWT speed. Cycle 5 min low load. Leg press 2×10 @ 50% 1-RM, seated row 2×10. PLB throughout. Stretch 5 min.
- **S3:** Treadmill 12 min same speed. Cycle 7 min. Add knee extension 2×10 @ 50% 1-RM, biceps curl 2×10. ACBT if sputum. Borg 4–5.
- **S4:** Treadmill 15 min @ 75% 6MWT speed. Cycle 8 min. Resistance 2×12. Add calf raises 2×10. Stair step 1×1 min if tolerated. SpO2 check at 5/10/15 min.
- **S5:** Begin interval option if SpO2 drops <88% on continuous: 1 min walk : 1 min rest × 12. Otherwise 18 min continuous @ 75–80%. Resistance 3×10. Add lat pulldown 2×10.
- **S6:** 20 min aerobic. Resistance 3×10, +5–10% load on tolerated exercises (RPE ≤6 last rep). Add unsupported arm exercise (1-kg dowel raises 2×10).
- **S7:** Aerobic 22 min @ 80% 6MWT speed. Re-test 1-min sit-to-stand. Resistance 3×10 progressed. Pacing for shower/dressing.
- **S8 (Mid-program review):** Repeat 6MWT, CAT. Re-prescribe at 80% new speed. Aerobic 25 min. Resistance 3×10–12.
- **S9:** Aerobic 25 min continuous (or 2×12 min intervals, 2-min recovery). Balance: tandem stance 3×30 s, single-leg stance 3×15 s. Resistance 3×12.
- **S10:** Aerobic 27 min. +5–10% resistance if last set RPE ≤6. Begin home walking program (pedometer, +500 steps/wk).
- **S11:** Aerobic 30 min @ 80% peak speed. Stair circuit 2×1 min. Resistance 3×12. Reinforce ACBT independence.
- **S12:** 30 min aerobic. Resistance 3×12 with reduced rest (40 s). Reassess sit-to-stand, handgrip.
- **S13:** 30 min aerobic + arm ergometer 5 min. Functional task (carry-and-walk, sit-to-stand with object).
- **S14:** Aerobic 30–35 min. Resistance 3×12 progressed. Transition planning (community gym, maintenance, home).
- **S15:** Mostly self-directed under supervision. Troubleshoot home program. Review exacerbation action plan.
- **S16 (Discharge):** Final 6MWT, CAT, mMRC, sit-to-stand, handgrip. Document MCID achievement (≥30 m 6MWT; ≥2 CAT). Provide 12-week home program + action plan.

### 7. Progression
- +2–3 min aerobic if Borg ≤5 and SpO2 ≥88% at current load.
- +5–10% intensity (speed/W) when Borg <4 at current dose.
- +5–10% resistance load when last set RPE ≤6 for two consecutive sessions.
- Add intervals if continuous work cannot be sustained ≥10 min due to dyspnea.

### 8. Stop / hold during a session
- SpO2 <85% despite supplemental O2
- Borg dyspnea ≥8 not relieved by 2-min rest + PLB
- HR drop >10 bpm with workload increase, or sustained HR >85% age-predicted max
- SBP rise >250 or fall >10 with ischemic features; DBP >115
- New chest pain, cyanosis, lightheadedness, ataxia, mental status change
- New arrhythmia, wheeze unresponsive to bronchodilator

### 9. Discharge / outcomes
- Δ6MWD ≥25–35 m (MCID); Δ CAT ≥2; Δ mMRC ≥1
- Independent with home program and inhaler technique
- Action plan in place; smoking cessation engaged

### 10. Special precautions
- **O2 titration:** SpO2 88–92% during exercise; avoid hyperoxia in CO2-retainers.
- **Exacerbation:** ↑ dyspnea, sputum volume/purulence — pause program, escalate; re-enter post-exacerbation PR within 4 wk of hospitalization (strong recommendation, ATS 2023 CPG).
- **Pre-bronchodilator** SABA 15–30 min before exercise if prescribed.
- Cold air, pollution, high humidity may worsen — modify environment.

## Condition: Asthma

### 1. Pathophysiology relevant to physio
- Variable, reversible airflow obstruction from inflammation, smooth muscle hyperresponsiveness, mucus plugging.
- **EIB:** airway cooling/drying during high minute ventilation triggers mediator release; peak FEV1 fall typically 5–15 min **after** stopping; ≥10% fall = EIB (ATS 2013 EIB CPG).
- Many patients are deconditioned → higher minute ventilation at submaximal work → more EIB (feedback loop).
- Comorbidities: allergic rhinitis, GERD, obesity, vocal cord dysfunction, anxiety, atopic dermatitis.

### 2. Assessment battery
- **Subjective:** ACT (≥20 well-controlled), ACQ-5/7, Nijmegen (dysfunctional breathing), Borg, exacerbations/ED visits, ICS adherence, trigger inventory.
- **Objective:** HR, BP, SpO2, RR, auscultation (wheeze), accessory muscle use, posture, PEF pre/post bronchodilator, chest expansion, breathing pattern (apical vs diaphragmatic, sigh frequency).
- **Functional:** 6MWT, ISWT, eucapnic voluntary hyperpnea or exercise challenge if EIB unclear, sit-to-stand.
- **Strength:** handgrip, quadriceps; consider MIP/MEP.
- **Red flags:** PEF <60% personal best, ACT <15, current wheeze with SpO2 <94%, recent ED visit (defer high-intensity).

### 3. Goals
- Achieve & maintain control (ACT ≥20).
- Reduce EIB severity through conditioning + warm-up routines.
- Restore breathing pattern (nasal, diaphragmatic, slow).
- Improve aerobic capacity to general norms; reduce reliever use.

### 4. FITT prescription (GINA 2025; EAACI 2025; ATS 2013)

**Aerobic:** F 3–5 d/wk · I 60–80% HRmax or RPE 4–7 (CR-10); intermittent often better-tolerated initially · T 30–60 min (incl warm-up) · Type: brisk walking, cycling, swimming (warm humid air friendly), elliptical; intermittent sports preferred over sustained high-ventilation cold/dry running.

**Resistance:** 2–3 d/wk, 60–80% 1-RM, 2–3 × 8–12.

**Breathing retraining (daily):** Papworth (paced nasal-diaphragmatic, relaxation), Buteyko (reduced minute volume), slow nasal breathing.

**Pre-exercise warm-up (specific to EIB):** induces 1–2 h refractory period — 6–8 × 30-sec near-maximal sprints with 1.5-min rests; OR continuous moderate warm-up at 60% VO2max for 15 min.

### 5. Session structure
- **Pre-session:** PEF check; SABA 15 min before if prescribed; nasal breathing cue.
- **Warm-up (15 min):** graded intervals (above) — critical for EIB refractoriness.
- **Conditioning (30–40 min):** aerobic + resistance circuit; nasal breathing where possible.
- **Cool-down (10 min):** gradual taper (avoid abrupt stop, which prolongs cooling/drying); Papworth diaphragmatic breathing.
- **Breathing block (10 min):** PLB not standard for asthma; use nasal slow breathing 6/min, diaphragmatic breathing supine, "rectangular" 4-2-4-2.

### 6. Session-by-session program (8 sessions over 8 weeks; +2–3 home/wk)

- **S1 (Assessment / Education):** ACT, Nijmegen, PEF, 6MWT, sit-to-stand. Inhaler/spacer technique, trigger map, written asthma action plan reviewed. Nasal-diaphragmatic breathing (Papworth-style) 10 min. Borg ≤3.
- **S2:** PEF pre. EIB-prevention warm-up: 6×30-sec brisk cycle sprints, 90-s recovery. Continuous cycle 15 min @ 60% HRR. Leg press, chest press, row 2×10 moderate. Breathing retraining 10 min. PEF post; document % drop.
- **S3:** Sprint warm-up. Aerobic 18 min @ 65% HRR. Resistance 2×12. Add scapular setting/postural drill (chin tucks, wall angels) ×10. Reinforce nasal breathing during low-intensity blocks.
- **S4:** Sprint warm-up. Treadmill intervals 1:1 (1 min @ 75% HRmax : 1 min recovery) × 10. Resistance 3×10 progressed. Buteyko-style control pause practice.
- **S5:** Sprint warm-up. Continuous 25 min cycle/treadmill @ 70% HRR. Resistance 3×10. Intro swimming/aquatic option if available. Reassess Nijmegen, PEF stability.
- **S6:** Sprint warm-up. Aerobic 30 min mixed. Resistance 3×12 progressed. VCD screen if symptoms inspiratory.
- **S7:** Sprint warm-up. Aerobic 30–35 min @ 75% HRR. Resistance circuit 3×12, 30-sec rest. Outdoor-conditions discussion (scarf/mask cold/dry, indoor alternatives, pollen times).
- **S8 (Discharge):** Repeat ACT, Nijmegen, 6MWT, sit-to-stand. Document outcomes. 12-week home program with EIB warm-up template. Confirm action plan, SABA pre-exercise rule, ICS adherence.

### 7. Progression
- Advance intensity/duration if PEF post-exercise drop <10% and ACT stable/improving.
- Hold/regress if reliever use increases, ACT drops ≥3, or PEF post-exercise drops ≥15%.

### 8. Stop / red flags
- PEF <60% best
- Wheeze unrelieved by 2 puffs SABA after 10 min
- SpO2 <92%
- Speech in single words, accessory muscle use
- Chest tightness with HR/BP instability
- Cyanosis, drowsiness — emergency referral

### 9. Discharge / outcomes
- ACT ↑ ≥3 (MCID); 6MWD ↑ ≥30 m
- Reduced SABA use; reduced post-exercise FEV1/PEF fall
- Independent with EIB warm-up routine

### 10. Special precautions
- **Pre-exercise SABA** 15 min before for known EIB.
- Avoid cold/dry air; scarf/mask; indoor alternatives in winter / high pollen.
- Maintain ICS / ICS-formoterol therapy (GINA 2025).
- Always exercise with rescue inhaler accessible.
- Asthma action plan reviewed every session.

## Condition: Post-CABG / Median Sternotomy

### 1. Pathophysiology relevant to physio
- Surgical insult: median sternotomy + sternal wires, CPB effects (atelectasis, pleural effusion, post-perfusion lung), graft sites (LIMA, saphenous, radial), pericardial inflammation.
- Acute exercise limits: pain, restrictive ventilatory pattern, atelectasis V/Q mismatch, beta-blockade blunting HR, deconditioning, possible perioperative MI / AF.
- Sternal healing: ~6–8 wk for bony union. **Keep Your Move in the Tube (KYMITT)** replaces older arbitrary "no lifting >5 lb / no shoulder >90°" rules with movement-load-symptom guidance — keep elbows close to torso (the imaginary tube), avoid asymmetric loaded reaches, guided by pain/clicking.
- Comorbidities: CAD, T2DM, HTN, dyslipidemia, CKD, prior MI, depression.

### 2. Assessment battery
- **Subjective:** pain (NRS sternum, leg harvest, shoulders), Borg, NYHA, dyspnea, sleep, mood (PHQ-9), DASI.
- **Objective:** HR, BP (often beta-blocked — use RPE), SpO2, RR, auscultation, sternal exam (instability, click, drainage), incision check, peripheral edema, pacemaker/ICD location, telemetry rhythm.
- **Functional:** 6MWT (often wk 2–4 once stable), sit-to-stand, gait speed, stairs if appropriate, DASI.
- **Strength:** handgrip; defer 1-RM testing for ≥6–8 wk post-op upper body; lower body knee extension can be tested earlier.
- **Red flags:** sternal click/instability, new arrhythmia, wound dehiscence/drainage, post-op anemia (Hb <8), uncontrolled HF, fever, pleural effusion compromising oxygenation.

### 3. Goals
- **Phase I (inpatient days 1–5/7):** prevent post-op pulmonary complications, early ambulation, KYMITT education, ADL independence at discharge.
- **Phase II (outpatient wks 2–12):** ↑ functional capacity (target METs), risk-factor modification, return to driving (~4 wk), return to work (~6–12 wk).
- **Phase III/IV:** long-term lifestyle exercise, secondary prevention.

### 4. FITT prescription (AACVPR 6th ed; AHA/ACC; CSANZ)

**Phase I (inpatient):** F 1–2× daily · I RPE ≤11–13 / Borg ≤3; HR rest +20 bpm; BP within ±20 mmHg of resting · T 5–10 → 10–20 min by discharge · Type: bed mobility, marching, ankle pumps, AROM lower limbs, sub-90° AROM upper limbs (KYMITT), STS, hallway walking, stairs prior to discharge.

**Phase II (outpatient, 36 sessions / 12 wk):** F 3 supervised + 2 home/wk · I 40–60% HRR initial → 60–80% HRR mid; RPE 11–14 (Borg 6–20) / 3–5 (CR-10); HR rest +20–30 bpm · T 20–30 → 40–60 min · Type: treadmill, cycle, NuStep, arm ergometer (after sternal healing ≥6 wk + MD clearance), resistance ~5–8 wk post-op with light loads (1–3 lb upper initially).

**Phase III:** maintenance, community-based, 3–5 d/wk, RPE 11–14, lifestyle integration.

### 5. Session structure (Phase II)
- **Pre-vitals:** HR (telemetry early), BP, SpO2, weight, symptoms.
- **Warm-up (5–10):** low-intensity walking/cycling, AROM (KYMITT), gentle trunk rotation as tolerated.
- **Conditioning (20–40):** aerobic continuous or interval; resistance once cleared.
- **Cool-down (5–10):** slow walking, stretching (avoid Valsalva, avoid heavy posterior chain loading sternum), incentive spirometry if early phase.
- **Breathing:** deep breathing with splinted cough using pillow, incentive spirometer (10×/hr in early Phase I), progressive thoracic mobility within KYMITT.
- **Education:** KYMITT, sternal precautions, wound care, medication review, weight monitoring.

### 6. Session-by-session program

**Phase I — Inpatient (POD = post-op day):**
- **POD 1:** Bed mobility w/ KYMITT (log-roll legs, push from elbow not palm). Ankle pumps 10/hr awake. Splinted cough. Incentive spirometer 10 breaths/hr. Sit edge of bed → chair if stable. Continuous vitals. Target: SpO2 ≥92%, HR rest +20 max.
- **POD 2:** STS ×3–5. In-room walks 2–3× day (50–100 ft). AROM ankles/knees/hips; sub-90° shoulder AROM, elbows in tube. Pain control before mobilization.
- **POD 3:** Hall 100–200 ft × 2–3. Standing AROM. Marching in place. Reinforce KYMITT for ADLs (combing hair within tube). Splinted cough q4h.
- **POD 4:** Hall 300–400 ft × 3. Seated leg extensions 1×10 (no resistance). Stairs assessment if discharge near (1 flight w/ rail).
- **POD 5–7 (discharge):** Hall 500–800 ft × 2. Stairs 1 flight independently. Provide home walking plan (5–10 min, 2–3×/day, RPE ≤11). Driving restriction 4 wk. No lifting >5–10 lb 6 wk (or KYMITT load-by-symptom). Phase II referral wk 2–4.

**Phase II — Outpatient (36 sessions, 3×/wk × 12 wk):**
- **S1 (~wk 2–3):** Full assessment incl 6MWT (sub-max; stop SpO2 <90% or RPE >13), telemetry baseline, BP, weight log. Treadmill 10 min @ 1.5–2.0 mph, RPE 11–12. Cycle 5 min low load. Lower-body stretching only. KYMITT detail. Weight monitoring (call MD if +2 kg/3 d). Wound check.
- **S2:** Treadmill 12 min, RPE 11–13. Cycle 8 min. Upper-body AROM in tube. NO upper-body resistance.
- **S3:** Treadmill 15 min. Cycle 10 min. Seated lower-body AROM 1×10. HR cap = rest +20 bpm.
- **S4 (wk ~3):** Aerobic 20 min split treadmill+cycle. NuStep 5 min. RPE 12–13.
- **S5:** Aerobic 22 min. Begin lower-body resistance: leg press 1×10 light, calf raise 1×10, seated knee extension 1×10. NO upper-body load.
- **S6 (wk ~4):** Aerobic 25 min. Lower-body resistance 2×10. Light walking poles for posture (no pull).
- **S7:** Aerobic 25 min @ RPE 13. Resistance 2×12 lower body. Driving clearance discussion with MD.
- **S8 (wk ~5):** Aerobic 30 min. Add arm ergometer 3 min very light if pain-free and ≥5 wk post-op. Sternum check.
- **S9:** Aerobic 30 min. Arm ergometer 5 min light. Lower-body resistance 2×12 progressed.
- **S10 (wk ~6, sternal union expected):** Repeat 6MWT. Begin upper-body resistance — chest press / row at 1–3 lb or 5% body weight, 1×10 each, only if no sternal click and surgeon-cleared. KYMITT respected.
- **S11:** Aerobic 30–35 min. Upper-body resistance 1×10–12 light, lower 2×12.
- **S12 (wk ~7):** Aerobic 35 min including 2×3-min @ RPE 14. Resistance circuit 2×10–12 light.
- **S13:** Aerobic 35–40 min. Resistance progressed 5–10% if tolerated.
- **S14 (wk ~8):** Aerobic 40 min. Resistance 2×12 progressed. Functional task practice (timed STS, lift basket within KYMITT).
- **S15:** Repeat 6MWT (mid-program). Adjust prescription. Aerobic 40 min @ RPE 13–14. Resistance 2–3×10.
- **S16–S18 (wk 9):** Aerobic 40 min interval/continuous mix. Resistance 3×10 progressed. Stair climb 5–10 min.
- **S19–S24 (wk 10–11):** Aerobic 40–45 min, RPE 13–15 selectively. Resistance 3×10–12. Arm ergometer 10 min. Begin return-to-work conditioning.
- **S25–S30 (wk 11–12):** Aerobic 45 min including HIIT 4×4 min @ 80% HRR if HF-free and cleared. Resistance 3×12. Phase III transition planning.
- **S31–S35:** Maintain volume/intensity; coach independence; pedometer/HR-monitor self-management.
- **S36 (Discharge):** Final 6MWT, STS, DASI, PHQ-9, lipid/BP/weight review. Phase III/maintenance program.

### 7. Progression
- +2–5 min aerobic or +0.2 mph or +5–10 W when RPE ≤12 at current dose for 2 sessions.
- +5 bpm HR target increments toward 80% HRR by mid-program.
- Resistance: +5–10% load when last set RPE ≤6 (CR-10) for two sessions.
- Hold if angina, new arrhythmia, fall in SBP >10 with workload, sternal symptoms.

### 8. Stop / red flags
- Angina or anginal-equivalent (jaw, arm, epigastric)
- SBP fall ≥10 mmHg with ischemic signs / increasing workload
- SBP >250 or DBP >115
- New ST changes, sustained VT, ≥2nd-degree AV block, multifocal PVCs increasing
- Sternal click, popping, instability, drainage, fever
- HR drop >10 bpm with workload
- Borg dyspnea ≥7 or RPE >17
- Patient request

### 9. Discharge / outcomes
- Δ6MWD typically +60–90 m by end of Phase II
- Achievement of vocational/lifestyle MET demand
- BP, lipids, weight, smoking targets met
- PHQ-9 improvement
- Independent home exercise + medication adherence

### 10. Special precautions
- **Sternal precautions / KYMITT:** elbows in the tube; avoid pushing/pulling >5–10 lb (or load-by-symptom) for 6–8 wk; no driving 4 wk; avoid Valsalva (no breath-holding lifts); no swimming until incision fully healed and surgeon-cleared.
- **Beta-blockade:** HR-based prescription unreliable — use RPE.
- **Post-op AF:** common; check rhythm before each session.
- **Saphenous harvest leg:** compression stocking, leg elevation, monitor DVT.
- **Diabetes:** glucose pre/post; have fast-acting carbs available.

## Condition: Heart Failure (HFrEF and HFpEF)

### 1. Pathophysiology relevant to physio
- **HFrEF (LVEF ≤40%):** impaired contractility → low CO, neurohormonal activation (RAAS, SNS), peripheral vasoconstriction, skeletal-muscle myopathy ("muscle hypothesis").
- **HFpEF (LVEF ≥50%):** stiff LV, impaired relaxation, chronotropic incompetence, peripheral O2-extraction abnormalities — limitation often dominated by peripheral and diastolic factors.
- Exercise limits: low CO, blunted HR (especially on beta-blocker, in HFpEF), pulmonary congestion, peripheral muscle fatigue.
- Comorbidities: CAD, AF, CKD, T2DM, COPD, OSA, obesity, iron deficiency anemia, frailty.

### 2. Assessment battery
- **Subjective:** NYHA I–IV, KCCQ or MLHFQ, Borg, fatigue, orthopnea, PND, sodium/fluid adherence, daily weight log, SOB on dressing/showering.
- **Objective:** HR (rhythm, rate, AF common), BP supine and standing (orthostatic), SpO2, RR, JVP, lung crackles, S3, peripheral edema (pitting grade), weight, ICD/CRT presence, recent labs (BNP/NT-proBNP, eGFR, K+, Hb).
- **Functional:** 6MWT (MCID ~30 m), CPET if available (peak VO2 <14 mL/kg/min high risk; <10 advanced HF), gait speed, STS, SPPB, REHAB-HF battery.
- **Strength:** handgrip (sarcopenia), quadriceps.
- **Hold-day red flags:** weight gain >2 kg/3 d, new orthopnea/PND, resting HR >100, SBP <90 or symptomatic, new arrhythmia, increased edema, recent decompensation hospitalization (require ≥1–6 wk stabilization per HF-ACTION).

### 3. Goals
- **Short-term:** clinical stability, daily weight self-monitoring, decompensation education, achieve NYHA I–II symptom level on ADLs.
- **Long-term:** ↑ peak VO2 / 6MWD, reduce HF hospitalizations, ↑ KCCQ ≥5 (clinically meaningful), maintain medication-titration tolerance.

### 4. FITT prescription (ESC 2021 + 2023; AHA/ACC/HFSA 2022; HF-ACTION; AHA HFpEF Statement 2023; REHAB-HF)

**Aerobic (HFrEF MICT — primary):** F 3–5 d/wk · I 40–70% HRR or 50–80% peak VO2; RPE 11–14 / 3–5 (CR-10); on beta-blocker: HR rest +10–20 initial → +20–30 progressed; or RPE/talk test · T 10–15 → 30–45 min over 8–12 wk · Type: walking, treadmill, cycle, recumbent NuStep.

**HIIT (selected stable HFrEF, supervised):** e.g., 4×4 min at 85–95% HRpeak with 3-min active recovery at 60–70% — improves peak VO2 more than MICT in some studies; SMARTEX-HF: equivalent to MICT in long-term remodeling.

**Resistance:** F 2–3 d/wk · I 30–40% 1-RM start → 60–70% · 1–3 sets × 10–15 · leg press, knee extension, chest press, row, biceps/triceps; small muscle groups first to limit pressor response.

**HFpEF:** same MICT principles; emphasize peripheral conditioning (resistance + endurance), weight loss if obese, BP control. REHAB-HF model: integrated strength, balance, mobility, endurance × 60 min, 3×/wk × 12 wk.

**IMT:** 30% MIP if MIP <70% predicted.

### 5. Session structure
- **Pre-session:** weight vs yesterday/baseline, HR rhythm/rate, BP supine and standing, SpO2, RR, edema, symptoms (PND, orthopnea, fatigue).
- **Warm-up (10):** extended low-intensity to allow chronotropic ramp, AROM, diaphragmatic breathing.
- **Conditioning (20–40):** aerobic ± resistance; ECG/telemetry monitoring early sessions and for ICD patients.
- **Cool-down (10):** prolonged taper (avoid post-exercise hypotension), gentle stretching (avoid prolonged supine flat if orthopnea), seated leg pumps.
- **Education:** daily weights, sodium/fluid, medication adherence/titration, decompensation recognition.

### 6. Session-by-session program (12 wk, 3×/wk supervised — HF-ACTION/REHAB-HF model)

- **S1 (Assessment):** NYHA, KCCQ, 6MWT (sub-max), SPPB, handgrip, weight log review. Daily weighing protocol, sodium <2–3 g/day, fluid limit (often 1.5–2 L). Walk 10 min @ RPE 11. Diaphragmatic breathing 5 min.
- **S2:** Walk/treadmill 10 min @ HR rest +10 bpm, RPE 11. Cycle 5 min low load. Lower-body AROM 1×10. Continuous telemetry. SpO2 ≥92%.
- **S3:** Aerobic 12 min split treadmill/cycle. Begin resistance: leg press 1×10 @ 30% 1-RM, seated row 1×10 light. Balance: tandem stance 3×30 s.
- **S4 (wk 2):** Aerobic 15 min @ RPE 11–12. Resistance 1×12 (leg press, row, chest press light). HR cap = rest +15 bpm initially.
- **S5:** Aerobic 18 min. Resistance 2×10 progressed. Add knee extension. Reinforce daily weight log.
- **S6:** Aerobic 20 min. Resistance 2×10. Add calf raises, biceps curl 1-kg. STS 2×10.
- **S7 (wk 3):** Aerobic 22 min @ RPE 12. Resistance 2×12. Orthostatic BP rechecked.
- **S8:** Aerobic 25 min. Resistance 2×12 progressed (5–10%). Single-leg stance 3×15 s.
- **S9:** Aerobic 25 min. Resistance circuit 2×12. IMT introduced if MIP low (30% MIP × 30 breaths × 2/day home).
- **S10 (wk 4):** Aerobic 27 min. Resistance 2×12. Stair-step 1 min × 2.
- **S11:** Aerobic 30 min @ RPE 12–13, HR cap = rest +20 bpm. Resistance 3×10.
- **S12:** Aerobic 30 min. Resistance 3×10. Repeat STS and gait speed.
- **S13 (wk 5):** Aerobic 30 min including 2×3 min @ RPE 14 (interval intro). Resistance 3×10–12.
- **S14:** Aerobic 32 min. Resistance progressed 5–10%. Functional task: carry 2-kg load 20 m.
- **S15 (wk 5–6):** Mid-program 6MWT, KCCQ. Adjust intensity by new HRR/speed. Aerobic 35 min.
- **S16–S18 (wk 6):** Aerobic 35 min. Resistance 3×10 progressed. HIIT consideration in stable HFrEF: 4×2 min @ RPE 15 with 3-min active recovery (only if no decompensation, ICD threshold respected).
- **S19–S21 (wk 7):** Aerobic 35–40 min MICT or HIIT. Resistance 3×12. Balance circuit.
- **S22–S24 (wk 8):** Aerobic 40 min. Resistance progressed. Reassess SPPB. Vocational return.
- **S25–S27 (wk 9):** Aerobic 40 min, mix continuous and HIIT (4×4 if tolerated). Resistance 3×12 with 30-s rest.
- **S28–S30 (wk 10):** Aerobic 40–45 min. Resistance 3×12 progressed. Functional integration.
- **S31–S33 (wk 11):** Aerobic 45 min. Resistance 3×12. Transition planning — community/home, telehealth maintenance.
- **S34–S35 (wk 12):** Self-directed under supervision. Independence with HR/RPE self-monitoring.
- **S36 (Discharge):** Final 6MWT, KCCQ, SPPB, handgrip. Document outcomes (Δ6MWD ≥30 m MCID, ΔKCCQ ≥5). Home program; HF action plan; daily weight log template.

### 7. Progression
- Advance time before intensity in early phase.
- Advance intensity when RPE ≤12 at current dose, no symptoms, weight stable, no orthostasis.
- Add HIIT only in stable HFrEF (NYHA II–III) with ≥4 wk MICT base, MD clearance, ICD threshold known.
- Hold/regress: weight gain ≥2 kg/3 d, new orthopnea/PND, resting HR >10 bpm above baseline, ↑ edema, change in HF medications requiring re-titration.

### 8. Stop / red flags
- SBP fall ≥10 mmHg below resting with workload (especially with symptoms)
- SBP >180 / DBP >110 at peak
- New angina, severe dyspnea (Borg ≥7), dizziness, ataxia, confusion
- HR fall >10 bpm with increasing workload, sustained VT, rapid AF >130 bpm, new heart block, ICD shock or threshold-approaching rate
- SpO2 <90%
- New crackles audible at bases mid-session
- Patient request

### 9. Discharge / outcomes
- Δ6MWD ≥30 m (CHF MCID; range 30–43 m)
- Δ peak VO2 ≥1.0–1.5 mL/kg/min if CPET used
- ΔKCCQ ≥5
- NYHA class improvement by ≥1
- Reduction in HF hospitalizations
- Independent self-management

### 10. Special precautions
- **Daily weight monitoring:** notify clinician if +2 kg/3 d or +2.3 kg/wk.
- **Fluid/sodium restriction:** per HF clinic.
- **Medication awareness:** diuretics → orthostasis; beta-blockers → blunted HR; ARNi → hypotension on standing.
- **ICD considerations:** know shock threshold; cap exercise HR to 20 bpm below ICD detection rate; avoid heavy upper-extremity loading on ipsilateral side for first 6 wk post-implant.
- **AF:** monitor rhythm, use RPE not HR if rate variable.
- **Anticoagulation:** avoid contact / high-fall-risk activities.
- **Decompensation signs:** ↑ dyspnea, orthopnea, PND, ankle swelling, weight gain, fatigue — pause exercise, refer.

### Universal stop-criteria (apply to all four conditions)

Stop the session immediately and reassess if any of the following:

- **Cardiac:** new chest pain or anginal equivalent; SBP fall ≥10 mmHg with ↑ workload + symptoms; SBP >250 or DBP >115; HR fall >10 bpm with rising workload; sustained VT, new heart block, multifocal PVCs increasing, ICD shock or rate approaching detection threshold; new or rapid AF >130 bpm.
- **Respiratory:** SpO2 <85% (COPD on O2), <90% (HF/CABG), or <92% (asthma) despite intervention; severe dyspnea Borg ≥8 not relieved by 2-min rest; audible wheeze unresponsive to SABA (asthma); new accessory muscle use with cyanosis.
- **Neurologic:** dizziness, ataxia, syncope, near-syncope, confusion, visual disturbance.
- **Surgical/sternal (CABG):** sternal click, pop, instability, drainage, fever, severe incisional pain.
- **Decompensation (HF):** new crackles, weight gain ≥2 kg/3 d, orthopnea, PND, ↑ edema mid-program.
- **General:** severe fatigue (RPE >17 / Borg 9–10 unrelenting), nausea/vomiting, claudication ≥3/4, leg pain or swelling suspicious for DVT, patient request.
- **Always defer the session** if: febrile, hypoglycemic episode in last 2 h, uncontrolled BP at rest (>180/110), or new or worsening symptoms not yet evaluated.

Document each stop event with timestamp, vitals, intervention given, response, and disposition (resume reduced intensity / terminate session / urgent referral / 911).
